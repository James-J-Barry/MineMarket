# M10: Modern finance (ATM, Bank Card, Pocket ATM, Brokerage Terminal)

Source: `docs/design.md` → progression table (Tier 8), "Item and block catalog" (Tier 8 rows), "Information as
progression" (Tier 8 row), "Securities as items" (custody), milestone M10.
Design doc "Done when": securities convert between paper and book entry both ways.

Goal: the player learns **dematerialization**: money and securities stop being things you carry and become entries in
an account you can reach from anywhere. That's convenient, and it changes what "owning" means: a book entry can't be
dropped or stolen, but it's only as good as the institution keeping the books.

Guiding principles (James): fun first but grounded; information in block screens; physical first until the player
pays to leave it behind.

## Decisions (James, 2026-09-25: "follow your own recommendations"; all recommendations below accepted)

1. **Split.** **M10a** = ATM, Bank Card and Pocket ATM. **M10b** = the Brokerage Terminal (book-entry custody, automatic
   income, trading every market from one block).

2. **ATM.** A block anyone can place anywhere; with your **Bank Card** in hand it opens your Bank Vault account's
   Account tab: deposit, withdraw, balance, Passbook. CDs and loans stay at the vault (the branch). Your vault must
   still stand somewhere: the ATM reaches your account, not a new one.

3. **Bank Card.** Crafted (blueprint with the ATM node). Needed to use an ATM. With the **Pocket ATM** perk, right-click
   the card anywhere to open the same Account tab, like an ender chest. A card is not an account: losing it costs the
   materials, never the money (unlocks and balances are keyed by account).

4. **Brokerage Terminal.** A block with three tabs:
   - **Holdings**: your book entries (shares by company, bonds by series, options by series) at today's value. **Deposit**
     moves every paper you carry into book entry (shares' unpaid dividends and bonds' due coupons are paid first);
     **Withdraw** prints papers back out (shares in the fewest certificates, bonds and options by series).
   - **Cash**: the brokerage cash account: dividends, coupons, maturities and option settlements on book entries are
     credited automatically at dawn (no presenting papers). Withdraw it in bills.
   - **Markets**: open the Stock Exchange, Bond Desk, Options Desk, Clearing House or Trading Floor screen from here.
     Papers bought still arrive as papers; deposit them.
5. **Records Terminal.** The Brokerage Terminal is linkable; linked, its book entries and cash count in net worth.
6. **Unlocks** (design doc): ATM **$300,000**, Pocket ATM **$200,000** (after ATM), Brokerage Terminal **$500,000**.
   Component **Computer Chip** ($1,500, depth 4). Blueprints: ATM = 1 Computer Chip, 1 Display Screen, 1 Lock
   Mechanism, 4 iron blocks; Bank Card = 1 Computer Chip, 1 gold; Brokerage Terminal = 2 Computer Chips, 2 Display
   Screens, 1 Circuit Board, 2 Engraved Plates, 8 planks. Balance deferred (James).
7. **Quests and guides.** Quests: **Cashless** (use an ATM; $200), **Paperless** (put papers into book entry; $300).
   Guides: **Electronic Money** (the ATM node: balances as records, cards, what a bank run is), **Book Entry** (the
   Brokerage node: custody, automatic income, why paper was dangerous and what book entry trusts instead).
