# M9: Options (Options Desk, calls and puts, the Greeks, writing options against collateral, the Volatility Board)

Source: `docs/design.md` → progression table (Tier 7), "Item and block catalog" (Tier 7 rows), "Contracts and item
collateral" (written options, covered positions), "Information as progression" (Risk Report Module), milestone M9.
Design doc "Done when": a covered call pays the full premium; a naked call pays less with weak collateral.

Goal: the player learns **optionality**: paying a small price for the right, not the obligation, to buy or sell later.
A put is insurance for a farmer; a call is a cheap, limited-loss bet. Then the other side: **writing** options earns
the premium but carries the risk, and what backs the promise changes what it's worth.

Guiding principles (James): fun first but grounded in real investing; information in block screens, not tooltips;
nothing risk-free that pays more than the vault; every counterparty reacts to volume.

Existing code to build on: `futures/ClearingHouse` (goods, lots, quarterly expiries, forward prices), `rates`
(the central bank's rate for discounting), `collateral/CollateralValuer` (liquidation value, quality Q), the Bond Desk
(papers in stackable series, presented for payment), the Records Terminal.

## Decisions (James, 2026-09-25: "follow your own recommendations"; all recommendations below accepted)

1. **Split.** **M9a** = the Options Desk for buying and selling back calls and puts, pricing, the Greeks and a payoff
   table on the desk's screen, settlement at expiry, two guides, two quests. **M9b** = writing options against
   collateral (covered and naked), the Volatility Board, the Risk Report Module, a guide, quests, `sim options`.

2. **Underlyings and sizes.** The six futures goods in the same lots (wheat 256, oak log 256, iron 64, gold 64,
   redstone 256, diamond 16) and the six companies' shares in contracts of 10 shares. One contract = one paper.

3. **Style and settlement.** European, **cash-settled**: at the expiry dawn the desk records each underlying's
   settlement price (a good's fair value, a share's last trade); present in-the-money papers any time after expiry
   and the desk pays their intrinsic value. Papers are bearer papers in stackable series (underlying, call or put,
   strike, expiry), like bonds.

4. **Expiries and strikes.** The next two quarter days (like futures). Five strikes per expiry around the forward
   price (80, 90, 100, 110, 120%), rounded to a tidy step; a series stays valid after the desk stops listing it.

5. **Price.** Black-76 on the forward (a good's futures price; a share's price grown at the central rate), discounted
   at the central bank's rate. Volatility = the underlying's realized volatility over the last 20 dawns (the desk
   records closes; a class default until it has 5), times a **smile**: 1 + 0.8 x ln(K/F)^2. The desk sells at fair
   +4% and buys back at fair -4% (at least a dime either way), and adds 0.5% per contract already bought or sold of
   that series today (it reacts to volume).

6. **The Greeks on the screen** (not on the paper): for the selected series, the payoff table (what it pays if the
   underlying ends at 70-130% of today), delta ("moves like N wheat"), theta ("loses $x a day if nothing moves") and
   vega ("$x per point of volatility"). Gamma waits for the Risk Report Module.

7. **Writing (M9b).** At the desk, place collateral in its slots and write calls or puts. Premium received = fair x
   (1 - 4%) x (1 - 0.3 x (1 - Q)), where Q is the collateral quality (`CollateralValuer`); the underlying itself backing
   a call counts as Q = 1 (**covered**), so a covered call earns the full premium. Written options are obligations on
   the account (like loans), with the collateral in escrow. Coverage: at opening the collateral's value C must cover
   the option's worst plausible loss (20% move) x 1.25; checked at each dawn like loans (margin call, then liquidation).
   At expiry: a covered call in the money is **called away** (the goods go, the strike is paid); other written options
   pay their intrinsic value out of the escrow. Escrow returns after settlement.

8. **Volatility Board (M9b)**, a wall block: implied volatility by strike for one underlying (the smile), and realized
   volatility, updating live.

9. **Risk Report Module (M9b)**: an upgrade item placed in a Records Terminal's module slot; adds a Risk tab: coverage
   per contract (loans, written options, futures margin), distance to each margin call, portfolio delta and vega per
   underlying, and a stress test ("what if wheat falls 20%?").

10. **Unlocks** (design doc): Options Desk **$150,000** (Tier 7), Volatility Board **$80,000**, Risk Report Module
    **$60,000** (requires Digital Record Keeping). Blueprints: Options Desk = 2 Engraved Plates, 1 Display Screen, 1
    Circuit Board, 4 emeralds, 8 planks; Volatility Board = 1 Display Screen, 2 Clockwork Gears, 4 iron; Risk Report
    Module = 1 Circuit Board, 1 Display Screen, 2 gold. Balance deferred (James).

11. **Quests and guides.**
    - M9a quests: **Insured** (a put pays out at expiry; $100), **Long Shot** (an option pays at least 3x what you paid;
      $150). M9b: **Covered Call** (write a covered call; $100), **Premium Collector** (a written option expires
      worthless; $120).
    - Guides: **Options** (calls, puts, premium, the payoff's shape; granted by the node), **The Greeks** (delta, theta,
      vega in game terms; granted by Insured), M9b: **Covered and Naked** (writing, collateral quality, being called
      away), **Volatility** (realized vs implied, the smile; granted by the Volatility Board).

12. **Sim** (`sim options`, M9b): buying calls and puts vs the underlying; a covered-call writer vs holding; a naked
    writer's premium with strong and weak collateral (the design doc's done-when); insurance for the farmer.

## What to build

### 1. Core (`exchange-core`, unit tests first)
- `options/OptionMath`: Black-76 price and Greeks, the normal CDF.
- `options/OptionDesk`: series, strikes, smile, quotes with volume skew, intrinsic value; realized volatility from
  recorded closes; settlement prices; save format.
- M9b: `options/WrittenBook` (written options, escrow, coverage, dawn checks, settlement).

### 2. Mod layer
- M9a: Options Desk block and screen (underlying, call/put, expiry, strike; price, Greeks, payoff table; buy, sell back,
  collect), `OptionsService` (dawn closes, settlement prices, papers, saves), Option Contract paper.
- M9b: writing tab with collateral slots, Volatility Board, Risk Report Module and the Records Terminal's Risk tab.
