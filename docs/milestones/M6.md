# M6: Equities (Stock Exchange, Share Certificates, companies, Electronic Newsfeed, Portfolio Binder, Safe Deposit Box)

Source: `docs/design.md` → progression table (Tier 4), "Item and block catalog" (Tier 4 rows), "Securities as items",
"Fictional companies", "Components" (Engraved Plate), "Information as progression" (Tier 4 row), milestone M6.
Design doc "Done when": dividends pay on presentation; earnings react to commodity prices.

Goal: the first thing the player can **own** that pays them. Six fictional companies earn money from the same
commodity prices the player has been trading, report every quarter (7 in-game days) and pay dividends. Shares trade on
a Stock Exchange that works like the Trading Floor, as **bearer certificates** you can hold, stack, store, lose and
sell. Tier 3 taught short-term trading; Tier 4 teaches that a share is a claim on a business, and that its price is
the market's guess about future earnings.

Guiding principles carried over from M5 (James): fun first but grounded in real investing; information lives in
block screens, not tooltips; the player should be able to make money investing, but never risk-free.

Existing code to build on: `exchange-core/exchange` (batch auction, custody, price history), `agents` (market maker,
noise, fundamentalist, momentum traders, `TradingFloor` tickets and receipts), `registry/SecurityRegistry` (serials
for CDs and loan notes), `dealer/WorldEvents` (news), the Trading Floor, Ticker Tape and Newsstand screens.

## Decisions (answered by James, 2026-09-24: all recommendations accepted)

1. **Split the milestone?** Recommended: **M6a** = companies, Stock Exchange, Share Certificates, dividends, the
   company report screen, guides and quests (playable on its own). **M6b** = Electronic Newsfeed, Portfolio Binder,
   Safe Deposit Box (information and custody). Alternative: all at once.

2. **How a company earns (the core model).** Recommended, per company, once a quarter (every 7 in-game days):
   - **Revenue** = its output x the average Dealer fair value of its commodities over the quarter (e.g. DSMC sells
     iron, gold and diamonds; GHF wheat, carrots and potatoes). So a drought or a crash in iron shows up in earnings,
     and a player flooding the iron market really does hurt Deepslate Mining.
   - **Costs** = fixed costs + input costs (e.g. NRP's iron), so earnings swing more than revenue (operating leverage).
   - **Output** grows at the company's growth rate, with a random quarterly surprise (the business itself, not the
     market).
   - **Dividend** = payout ratio x earnings (zero if earnings are negative; RSD pays none).
   Companies and their numbers live in a data file (`companies.csv`), like the Floor and Dealer catalogs.

3. **The six companies** (design doc; names still placeholders). Recommended mapping to things the game models:

   | Ticker | Earnings driven by | Profile |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | DSMC Deepslate Mining | iron, gold, diamond prices | cyclical, pays dividends |
   | GHF Golden Harvest Farms | wheat, carrot, potato prices; harvest/drought events | steady, weather shocks |
   | NRP Nether Rail & Portal | the Capital's trade volume (not just the player's crates); iron is its cost | growth, moderate risk |
   | ENCH Enchanted Arms | iron and emerald prices; raid events spike demand | event-driven, volatile |
   | RSD Redstone Dynamics | redstone price plus a big, noisy growth rate; no dividend | high risk, high expected return |
   | OWL Overworld Utility & Light | coal as its cost; stable regulated fees | bond-like, high dividend |

   The doc says NRP depends on "Trade Route Crate volume". In single player that is only the player's own crates;
   recommended: the Capital's overall shipping plus the player's, so it isn't zero for a player who never ships.

4. **What sets the share price.** Recommended: the same batch auction and NPC traders as the Floor, one book per
   company. The fundamentalists' anchor is a **fair value from expected dividends** (a simple dividend-discount model:
   next year's earnings x payout, growing at the growth rate, discounted at a **required return a bit above the
   vault**). That makes owning shares pay about the required return on average (dividends plus price growth), more
   than the vault but with real risk. Momentum and noise traders push the price around it; earnings surprises and
   company news move it. Target to check with a sim: a diversified holding earns about 1.5-2x the vault's 0.3% a day
   on average, with losing weeks and at least one company losing a third of its value in a bad quarter.

5. **How you buy and sell.** Recommended: the Stock Exchange screen works like the Trading Floor (one book per
   company, limit and market orders, Order Slips, repricing, fills and receipts in output slots), plus certificate
   handling. Buying fills pay out as **Share Certificates**; selling means placing certificates in the order. Reusing
   the Floor's engine keeps it familiar and cheap to build. Alternative: a simpler "buy/sell at the quote" screen.

6. **Certificates: bearer papers, and how they stack.** The doc: 1-, 10- and 100-share denominations, stacking, free
   split/merge at the exchange. Recommended:
   - Certificates are **fungible** within a company and denomination (like dollars), so they stack to 64 and carry no
     serial. The SecurityRegistry's one-serial-settles-once check doesn't fit fungible shares; dupes of shares are
     treated like dupes of dollars (out of scope, creative only).
   - The exchange **splits and merges** denominations for free.
   - Losing them on death is a real risk; the custody guide and the Safe Deposit Box (M6b) answer it.
   Alternative: serial-numbered certificates that never stack (more grounded, much more inventory clutter).

7. **Dividends: how they're paid.** Doc: "unclaimed dividends accrue on the paper and are paid when presented".
   Recommended: each certificate records the **last quarter it was paid through**. Presenting certificates at the
   Stock Exchange pays every dividend since then, in bills, and updates them. Certificates with different "paid
   through" quarters don't stack until presented (the exchange shows "dividends waiting"). A certificate bought after
   the quarter's record date doesn't earn that quarter's dividend (realistic ex-dividend drop in price).

8. **Company reports: item or screen?** The doc has an Annual Report *item* each quarter per holding. Following your
   preference for information in block screens: recommended, a **Report tab on the Stock Exchange screen** per
   company: last 4 quarters of revenue, costs, earnings and dividend, plus price/earnings and dividend yield. Free to
   read (fundamental analysis is the lesson). No item.

9. **Electronic Newsfeed (M6b).** Recommended: a Tier 4 block ($2,000) with a screen like the Newsstand's, for
   company news: earnings previews (a hint whether the coming report beats or misses), product launches, strikes,
   accidents. Like commodity news, the market hears a while after it appears, never stated. Commodity news stays on
   the Newsstand.

10. **Portfolio Binder and Safe Deposit Box (M6b).**
    - **Portfolio Binder** ($5,000, item): holds up to 27 security items (certificates, CDs, loan notes, later bonds),
      opens a screen listing each holding and the **total value at live prices**. That total is the purchased
      information (the doc's "Information as progression"). Certificates themselves show no value in their tooltip.
      This also answers the doc's open question on live values: live in the binder, none on the paper.
    - **Safe Deposit Box** ($4,000, block): 54 slots, only securities and currency, blast-proof, owner-only, and
      (like the Bank Vault) breakable only when empty. Shows no totals.

11. **Unlocks, costs and components.** Doc: Stock Exchange $10,000 (component **Engraved Plate**, $120, depth 8),
    Electronic Newsfeed $2,000, Portfolio Binder $5,000, Safe Deposit Box $4,000 (Lock Mechanism). Tier gate: half of
    Tier 3. Blueprints to propose (doc has none): Stock Exchange = 2 Engraved Plates + 4 gold + 1 Ledger Paper +
    planks; Newsfeed = 1 Clockwork Gear + 1 Engraved Plate + redstone; Binder = leather + Security Paper; Safe Deposit
    Box = iron blocks + 1 Lock Mechanism.
    **Balance note:** Tier 3 already takes ~19-20 h against ~10 h. At the doc's prices Tier 4 ($21,000 plus parts)
    would add many more hours of gathering; but Tier 4 is where investing income arrives. Recommended: build with
    the doc's prices, then run `sim progression` with a Tier 3 trader's income and a Tier 4 investor's dividends,
    and bring you numbers before tuning.

12. **Quests and guides.** The doc defines none for Tier 4. Recommended:
    - Quests: **Shareholder** (collect a dividend), **Buy the Business** (own 100 shares of one company), **Beat the
      Market** (sell shares for more than you paid, tracked from your own fills), **Diversified** (hold shares of 3
      companies at once; M6b if it needs the binder).
    - Guides: **Owning a Share** (ownership, dividends, record dates), **Valuing a Company** (earnings, P/E, dividend
      yield, required return), **Risk and Return** (why shares beat the vault on average and lose some weeks),
      **Custody** (M6b: bearer papers, the binder and the deposit box). Numbers pinned by tests.

13. **Short selling?** Not in the doc for Tier 4. Recommended: **no** (you can only sell certificates you hold).
    Shorting fits later with futures and options.

14. **NPC traders in the world.** As with the Floor, cosmetic villagers around the Stock Exchange: **park**.

## What to build (after decisions)

### 1. Core (`exchange-core`, unit tests first)
- `equities/Company`, `CompanyCatalog` (`companies.csv`), `Earnings` (quarterly revenue, costs, earnings, dividend
  from Dealer fair values, growth and surprises; seeded, reproducible), `Valuation` (dividend-discount fair value).
- `equities/StockExchange`: books per company on the batch auction with NPC populations anchored to fair value;
  tickets, receipts, custody of certificates and cash (reusing `TradingFloor`'s approach).
- Dividend ledger: quarter record dates, per-share dividends, "paid through" arithmetic.
- Persistence: company state (quarters reported, output, surprises), exchange, history.

### 2. Mod layer
- Items: Share Certificate (per company x 1/10/100, "paid through" data), Engraved Plate component.
- Stock Exchange block, menu and screen (books, orders, output, certificate split/merge, dividends, Report tab).
- Quarter tick: report earnings, record dividends, feed news (M6b).
- M6b: Electronic Newsfeed block/screen, Portfolio Binder item/screen, Safe Deposit Box block.

### 3. Balance
- `sim equities`: company earnings and prices over a simulated year; total return per company and for an even
  portfolio vs the vault; worst quarter; how much a player flooding a commodity moves the linked company.
- `sim progression` Tier 4 timing.
