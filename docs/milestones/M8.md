# M8: Forwards and futures (Forward Contracts, the Clearing House, daily mark-to-market, margin calls)

Source: `docs/design.md` → progression table (Tier 6), "Item and block catalog" (Tier 6 rows), "Contracts and item
collateral", "Counterparties in single player" (Clearing House), milestone M8, open question "Delivery vs cash
settlement".
Design doc "Done when": a wheat hedge offsets a price drop in sim.

Goal: the player learns to **lock in a price**. A farmer who sells next week's harvest forward today no longer cares
what wheat does in between; that is hedging. Then the Clearing House turns private deals into standard contracts that
anyone can trade any day, marked to market every dawn: the player learns margin, leverage, and what a margin call
feels like.

Guiding principles (James): fun first but grounded in real investing; information in block screens, not tooltips;
nothing risk-free that pays more than the vault; every counterparty reacts to volume.

Existing code to build on: `dealer/Dealer` (pricing, impact, inflation `priceLevel`), `collateral` (margin checks at
dawn), `BankService.dawn` (the dawn job pattern), the Basic Exchange menu, the Records Terminal (linkable blocks).

## Decisions (James, 2026-09-25: "follow your own recommendations"; all recommendations below accepted)

1. **Split.** **M8a** = Forward Contracts with the Dealer (a Forward tab at the Basic Exchange), the Hedging guide and
   quest. **M8b** = the Clearing House, futures, margin account, daily mark-to-market, Margin Call Notice, guides,
   quests, `sim hedge`. M8a is playable alone.

2. **Delivery vs cash settlement** (design doc open question). Recommended: **forwards are delivered** (you bring the
   goods; that's what a farmer's forward is), **futures are cash-settled** at the Dealer's fair value on expiry day
   (no logistics; you trade the price, not the wheat). Real commodity futures can be delivered, but almost all are
   closed or cash-settled first.

3. **Forward terms.** Recommended: sell-side only (a producer's hedge: "I will deliver N items on day T, you pay F").
   Any tradeable goods (not components), 16-1,024 items, delivery in **3, 7 or 14 days**. Speculating in both
   directions is what futures are for.

4. **Forward price.** Recommended: exactly what the Dealer would pay on delivery day if nothing surprising happens:
   today's fair value grown by inflation to day T, against the Dealer's inventory decayed to day T **plus every open
   forward on that item** (so ten forwards can't dodge price impact), with the player's usual spread (licensed or
   not). No extra fee: the price of certainty is giving up the upside. Delivery adds the goods to the Dealer's
   inventory like a sale (it moves the price).

5. **Deposit and default.** Recommended: signing takes **1 Security Paper** and a **20% deposit** in bills, returned on
   delivery. Deliver on day T or T+1 at any Basic Exchange; at dawn of T+2 an undelivered forward is cancelled and the
   deposit is forfeit. No daily margin: the lesson of forwards is that they are private, fixed, and can't be closed
   early. The contract is on the account (like a loan); a Forward Contract paper is its printed statement.

6. **Clearing House products.** Recommended: cash-settled futures on **6 goods** in standard lots: wheat (256), oak log
   (256), iron ingot (64), gold ingot (64), redstone (256), diamond (16). Two expiries listed at any time: the next two
   quarter days (multiples of 7). Futures price = the Dealer's fair value now, grown by inflation to expiry. The
   desk quotes **0.25% either side**, plus **0.1% per lot** the player has already traded in that contract today
   (resets at dawn): it reacts to volume. **Position limit 20 lots** per contract (like real exchanges; stops a player
   from cornering the settlement price).

7. **Margin.** Recommended: an account-keyed **margin account** at the Clearing House (deposit bills, withdraw what
   isn't needed). **Initial margin 10%** of a position's value, **maintenance 7.5%**. Every **dawn** the Clearing House
   **marks to market**: each position's gain or loss since the last mark moves in or out of the margin account in
   cash. Below maintenance: a **Margin Call Notice** (paper) and a chat message; top up to initial margin by the next
   dawn or every position in that account is closed at the dawn mark. A negative balance is a debt to the Clearing
   House: no new positions until it's paid. Leverage is about 10x: a 10% move doubles or wipes out the margin.

8. **Papers.** Recommended: futures positions live on the account (the Clearing House screen shows them); no paper per
   position, so the inventory stays clean. The Margin Call Notice is the one paper, since the design doc wants that
   moment to be physical. Forward Contracts print one paper each.

9. **Records Terminal.** Recommended: the Clearing House becomes linkable. Linked, it adds the margin account (cash, or
   debt when negative) and open futures gains or losses; forwards show as upcoming deliveries on the Calendar, with
   the deposit as an asset.

10. **Unlocks, costs and components** (design doc): Forward Contract **$40,000** (perk, like the CD), Clearing House
    **$80,000** (requires Forward Contract). No new components: Clearing House blueprint = 2 Engraved Plates, 1 Display
    Screen, 1 Clockwork Gear, 4 gold, 8 stone. Tier 6 gate: half of Tier 5. Balance deferred (James).

11. **Quests and guides.** Recommended:
    - Quests: **Locked In** (deliver a forward when the Dealer's bid is below your forward price; $60), **Marked to
      Market** (be paid variation margin at a dawn mark; $60), **Met the Call** (top up after a margin call before
      it's closed; $80), **Speculator** (close a futures position at a profit; $80).
    - Guides: **Hedging** (forwards: locking a price, giving up the upside, the deposit), **Futures and Margin**
      (standard lots, the Clearing House, mark-to-market, leverage), **Margin Calls** (maintenance, what happens at
      dawn, why leverage cuts both ways). Numbers pinned by tests.

12. **Sim.** `sim hedge`: a wheat farmer with a steady harvest over 20 weeks in 8 worlds, unhedged vs selling each
    harvest forward vs shorting futures: weekly income spread and worst week, and a forced 20% price drop to show the
    hedge offsetting it. Speculator: long futures at 10x, returns and worst week vs the vault.

## What to build

### 1. Core (`exchange-core`, unit tests first)
- `Dealer.quoteForward` (price at delivery with inventory decayed plus pending forwards) and `Dealer.absorb` (goods
  delivered into the Dealer's inventory).
- `contracts/Forward` (terms, deposit, due window, default) and `contracts/ForwardBook` (per account, save format).
- M8b: `futures/FuturesSpec` (products, lots, expiries), `futures/FuturesAccount` (positions, margin cash, marks,
  calls, liquidation, save format), `futures/ClearingHouse` (quotes with volume skew, position limits, dawn mark).

### 2. Mod layer
- M8a: Forward tab on the Basic Exchange (goods in the slot choose item and quantity; term buttons; price, deposit;
  Sign; Deliver; list of open forwards), `ForwardService` (dawn defaults, saves), Forward Contract paper.
- M8b: Clearing House block and screen (contracts, positions, margin), `FuturesService` (dawn marks and calls, saves),
  Margin Call Notice, Records Terminal link.

### 3. Balance
- `sim hedge`. `sim progression` Tier 6 timing.
