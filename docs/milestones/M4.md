# M4: Banking and item collateral (Bank Vault, Passbook, Certificate of Deposit, Loan Note)

Source: `docs/design.md` → progression table (Tier 2), "Item and block catalog" (Tier 2 rows), "Components"
(Lock Mechanism, Security Paper), "Securities as items" (serials, settlement, duplication), "Contracts and item
collateral" (valuation, haircuts, Q, worked example, margin calls), "Economy balance" (Bank Vault rate, advance rate),
"Technical architecture" (`contracts`, `collateral`, `registry` packages), milestone M4.
Goal: money can be **kept safe and earn interest** (Bank Vault), **locked up for a better rate** (CD), and
**borrowed against items** (Loan Note), with collateral valued at what the Dealer would really pay and margin calls
that liquidate through the Dealer. Design doc "Done when": the worked loan example matches the table; a margin call
liquidates correctly in a GameTest.

**Status: draft. The decisions below need James's answers before building.** Defaults marked *(rec.)* are what
I'd build if you just say "go".

## Decisions for James

1. **Split the milestone?** M4 is about twice M3's size.
   - *(rec.)* **Yes:** **M4a** = Bank Vault, Passbook, interest, CD, the `registry` package. **M4b** = collateral
     valuation, Loan Note, margin calls, liquidation. Each is playable on its own; M4a ships first.
2. **One account or one per vault?** The doc keys balances by account, but also says the Passbook covers "that
   vault only" and the Tier 8 ATM is what gives access from anywhere.
   - *(rec.)* **One bank account per player, reachable only at a Bank Vault they own.** A player may have one
     vault placed at a time; breaking it never loses money (the account lives on the player, not the block), and
     placing it elsewhere reconnects. The ATM later removes the "go to your vault" step.
3. **Passbook.** The doc says it's free with the vault and required to withdraw. You passed on items that do
   nothing, so:
   - *(rec.)* The Passbook is the **only way to see your balance and history** (information as progression): the
     vault screen shows deposits/withdrawals work, but the balance and the transaction list are read from the
     Passbook (right-click opens a book view). It is **not** required to withdraw. A lost one is replaced at the
     vault for 1 Ledger Paper.
   - Alternative: keep "required to withdraw" as a security flavor. Adds friction, teaches little.
4. **Interest timing.** *(rec.)* 0.3% per in-game day, compounded once per full day, credited lazily (computed
   from the last update when the account is touched or at dawn), so a paused world earns nothing. Interest is
   credited rounded **down to the dime**, like every other payout.
5. **CD terms** (the doc only says "a higher rate"). *(rec.)* **7 days at 0.45%/day** or **21 days at
   0.60%/day**, compounded daily, minimum $100, funded from the vault balance. Early redemption returns principal
   only. Each CD costs 1 Security Paper to issue. It's a **bearer paper** with a serial: whoever holds it can
   redeem it into their own vault, once.
6. **Loan Note and Collateral Receipt.** Debts are keyed by account (so losing the paper can't erase a debt), which
   makes a separate Collateral Receipt redundant.
   - *(rec.)* **One item, the Loan Note:** the borrower's statement. Tooltip and book view show amount owed, rate,
     collateral, coverage and the next margin check. Not required to repay (repay at the vault). A replacement costs
     1 Security Paper. No Collateral Receipt item.
7. **Loan rate: fixed or floating?** *(rec.)* **Floating:** recomputed each dawn from the current Q
   (0.4% + 1.0% × (1 − Q)), so collateral whose market you crash costs more to borrow against. The quote at
   opening shows today's rate.
8. **Collateral classes.** The doc's class A lists Netherite Ingot, which the Dealer doesn't trade (so it would be
   class D). *(rec.)* A new optional `collateral_class` column in the Dealer catalog: **A** diamond, emerald, gold;
   **B** other mining; **C** farm, mobs, wood/logs; **D** cobblestone, stone, sand, gravel, rotten flesh, components,
   anything not traded. Netherite stays out until it's in the catalog. Bills in escrow: class A (10% haircut) as the
   doc says.
9. **Margin Call Notice.** The doc lists it as a Tier 6 item but uses it for M4 loans. *(rec.)* No item: a chat
   message at dawn, the vault block's status light turns red, and the Loan Note tooltip shows the deadline.
10. **Where does loan cash go?** *(rec.)* Into the vault balance (withdraw as bills), so borrowing never drops
    money in the world by accident. Liquidation surplus returns there too; a shortfall becomes debt at the loan rate
    and blocks new loans until repaid (the doc's rule).
11. **"Save It" discount.** Quest 8 already grants `perk:bank_vault_discount_10`. *(rec.)* Implement it now: the
    Bank Vault node costs $450 instead of $500 for players holding the perk.
12. **Tier 2 quests.** The doc defines none. *(rec.)* Add three small ones so Tier 2 has the same "feel it, then
    read it" rhythm: **Nest Egg** (earn $10 of interest), **Locked In** (hold a CD to maturity), **Leverage**
    (open and fully repay a loan). $10–$25 rewards.
13. **Persistence.** The doc says SavedData/attachments; the project uses atomic text files. *(rec.)* Keep text
    files: `players/<uuid>.bank.txt` (account, history, debt, escrow) and `registry.txt` (serials). Same pattern as
    M2/M3, unit-tested formats.

## What to build

### 1. Core packages (`exchange-core`, pure Java, unit tests first)
- **`registry`**: `SecurityRegistry` issue(type, terms) → serial, `settle(serial)` once only (a second settle is
  rejected and the paper is marked VOID), text save format.
- **`contracts`**: `BankAccount` (balance, transaction log, lazy daily compounding), `Cd` (principal, rate, term,
  value at day n, early-redemption rule), `Loan` (principal, accrued interest, floating rate, repayment).
- **`collateral`**: `CollateralValuer` (P(n) via the Dealer, haircut by class, C, Q, max loan = C / 1.25,
  daily rate), `Escrow` (items held per account), `MarginCheck` (coverage ≥ 110% at dawn, one-day grace,
  liquidation order: largest haircut class first, sold through the Dealer, surplus/shortfall).
- Catalog: `collateral_class` column (decision 8); components **Lock Mechanism** ($60, depth 16) and **Security
  Paper** ($15, depth 48) at 40% spread.

### 2. Content
- Nodes (Tier 2, gate = half of Tier 1): **Bank Vault** $500 (grants blueprint + Passbook on first use; $450 with
  the Save It perk), **Certificate of Deposit** $1,200 (perk: issue CDs), **Loan Note** $2,000 (perk: borrow).
- Blueprint: Bank Vault = 8 Iron Blocks + 1 Lock Mechanism + 1 Ledger Paper (design doc).
- Guides (150–250 words, numbers pinned by tests): **Interest and Compounding**, **Term and Liquidity**,
  **Leverage and Collateral** (opens with the worked-example table).
- Quests per decision 12.

### 3. Mod layer
- **Bank Vault** block (owner-only like the crate): Deposit (bills from inventory and clips), Withdraw (to Bill
  Clips then inventory), CD tab (issue / redeem), Loan tab (post collateral into escrow, see C/Q/max loan/rate,
  borrow, repay, close). Status light: green / red during a margin call.
- **Passbook**, **Certificate of Deposit**, **Loan Note** items with data components (serial, terms) and a book-view
  screen; CDs are bearer papers checked against the registry.
- `BankService` (per-player accounts, dawn tick: interest, CD maturity, margin checks, liquidation), persistence per
  decision 13. The Almanac and exchanges keep paying from bills and clips (vault money must be withdrawn first).

### 4. Balance
- `sim progression` Tier 2 phase: time to afford Tier 2 (design target about 5 h total), vault interest vs income,
  and a scripted loan that gets margin-called when its collateral's price is dumped.
