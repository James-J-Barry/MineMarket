# M4: Banking and item collateral (M4a: Bank Vault, Passbook, CD. M4b: Loan Note, collateral, margin calls)

Source: `docs/design.md` → progression table (Tier 2), "Item and block catalog" (Tier 2 rows), "Components"
(Lock Mechanism, Security Paper), "Securities as items" (serials, settlement, duplication), "Contracts and item
collateral" (valuation, haircuts, Q, worked example, margin calls), "Economy balance" (Bank Vault rate, advance rate),
"Technical architecture" (`contracts`, `collateral`, `registry` packages), milestone M4.
Goal: money can be **kept safe and earn interest** (Bank Vault), **locked up for a better rate** (CD), and
**borrowed against items** (Loan Note), with collateral valued at what the Dealer would really pay and margin calls
that liquidate through the Dealer. Design doc "Done when": the worked loan example matches the table; a margin call
liquidates correctly in a GameTest.

## Decisions (answered by James)

1. **Split:** **M4a** = Bank Vault, Passbook, interest, CD, the `registry` package, quests Nest Egg and Locked In.
   **M4b** = collateral valuation, escrow, Loan Note, margin calls, liquidation, quest Leverage. M4a ships first.
2. **Accounts and the vault block:** one bank account per player, reachable only at a Bank Vault they own (one
   placed at a time). **The vault can only be broken when completely empty**: no balance, no loan or debt,
   nothing in escrow. (CDs are papers you carry, so they don't count; redeeming one needs a placed vault.) Moving it means withdrawing everything and carrying the cash yourself, so the Tier 8
   ATM stays the only remote access. It is also blast-proof, and only its owner can break it.
3. **Passbook:** the only place the balance and transaction history are shown (right-click opens a book view).
   Not required to withdraw. Replaced at the vault for 1 Ledger Paper.
4. **Interest:** 0.3% per in-game day, compounded once per full day, computed lazily (a paused world earns
   nothing), credited in whole dimes; each day's exact interest carries over, so small balances still earn.
5. **CDs:** 7 days at 0.45%/day or 21 days at 0.60%/day, compounded daily, minimum $100, funded from the vault
   balance, 1 Security Paper each. Early redemption returns principal only. Bearer paper with a serial: whoever
   holds it can redeem it into their own vault, once.
6. **Loan Note:** one item, the borrower's statement (amount owed, rate, collateral, coverage, next check). The
   debt lives on the account; the note isn't needed to repay. Replaced for 1 Security Paper. No Collateral Receipt.
7. **Loan rate:** floating, reset each dawn from the current Q: 0.4% + 1.0% × (1 − Q).
8. **Collateral classes:** a `collateral_class` catalog column. A: diamond, emerald, gold. B: other mining.
   C: farm, mobs, wood. D (refused): cobblestone, stone, sand, gravel, rotten flesh, components, anything not
   traded. Netherite stays out until the Dealer trades it. Bills in escrow: class A.
9. **Margin calls:** no notice item. A chat message at dawn, the vault's light turns red, the Loan Note shows the
   deadline.
10. **Loan cash** goes into the vault balance; liquidation surplus returns there; a shortfall becomes debt at the
    loan rate and blocks new loans until repaid.
11. **Save It:** the Bank Vault node costs $450 for players holding `perk:bank_vault_discount_10`.
12. **Tier 2 quests:** Nest Egg (earn $10 of interest), Locked In (hold a CD to maturity), Leverage (open and
    fully repay a loan), $10–$25 each.
13. **Persistence:** atomic text files, `players/<uuid>.bank.txt` and `registry.txt`, formats unit-tested.

## What to build

### 1. Core packages (`exchange-core`, pure Java, unit tests first)
- **`registry`**: `SecurityRegistry` issue(type, terms) → serial, `settle(serial)` once only (a second settle is
  rejected and the paper is marked VOID), text save format.
- **`contracts`**: `BankAccount` (balance, transaction log, lazy daily compounding), `Cd` (principal, rate, term,
  value at day n, early-redemption rule), `Loan` (principal, accrued interest, floating rate, repayment).
- **`collateral`**: `CollateralValuer` (P(n) via the Dealer, haircut by class, C, Q, max loan = C / 1.25,
  daily rate), `Escrow` (items held per account), `MarginCheck` (coverage ≥ 110% at dawn, one-day grace,
  liquidation order: largest haircut class first, sold through the Dealer, surplus/shortfall).
- Catalog: `collateral_class` column (decision 8); components **Lock Mechanism** ($60, depth 16) and **Security
  Paper** ($15, depth 48) at 40% spread.

### 2. Content
- Nodes (Tier 2, gate = half of Tier 1): **Bank Vault** $500 (grants blueprint + Passbook on first use; $450 with
  the Save It perk), **Certificate of Deposit** $1,200 (perk: issue CDs), **Loan Note** $2,000 (perk: borrow).
- Blueprint: Bank Vault = 8 Iron Blocks + 1 Lock Mechanism + 1 Ledger Paper (design doc).
- Guides (150–250 words, numbers pinned by tests): **Interest and Compounding**, **Term and Liquidity**,
  **Leverage and Collateral** (opens with the worked-example table).
- Quests per decision 12.

### 3. Mod layer
- **Bank Vault** block (owner-only like the crate; breakable only when completely empty, per decision 2): Deposit (bills from inventory and clips), Withdraw (to Bill
  Clips then inventory), CD tab (issue / redeem), Loan tab (post collateral into escrow, see C/Q/max loan/rate,
  borrow, repay, close). Status light: green / red during a margin call.
- **Passbook**, **Certificate of Deposit**, **Loan Note** items with data components (serial, terms) and a book-view
  screen; CDs are bearer papers checked against the registry.
- `BankService` (per-player accounts, dawn tick: interest, CD maturity, margin checks, liquidation), persistence per
  decision 13. The Almanac and exchanges keep paying from bills and clips (vault money must be withdrawn first).

### 4. Balance
- `sim progression` Tier 2 phase: time to afford Tier 2 (design target about 5 h total), vault interest vs income,
  and a scripted loan that gets margin-called when its collateral's price is dumped.
